STAGE 1: GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

1. Question Palette Color Legend

The Question Palette displayed on the right side of the screen will show the status of each question using the following official symbols:

1
You have not visited the question yet.
2
You have not answered the question.
3
You have answered the question.
4
You have marked for review (not answered).
5
The question is answered & marked for review (will be evaluated).
Important Note on Evaluation: Questions that are Answered and Marked for Review will be considered in the final score calculation. If an option is selected and marked for review, it will count as your submission for that question.

2. Navigating to a Question

  • Click on the question number in the Question Palette to jump directly to that question.
  • Note that using this option does NOT automatically save your answer to the current question.
  • Click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then proceed to the next question.
  • Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark the question for later review and move to the next question.

3. Answering Procedure for Multiple Choice Questions

  • To select your answer, click on the button corresponding to one of the options (A, B, C, or D).
  • To deselect your chosen answer, click on the chosen option again or click the Clear Response button.
  • To change your chosen answer, simply click on the button for another option.
  • To save your answer, you MUST click the Save & Next button.

4. Navigating Between Sections

  • Sections in this examination are displayed across the top bar. Questions in a section can be viewed by clicking on that section name.
  • Depending on the test configuration, sections may have dedicated timers or one-way locks. Please read the test-specific instructions on the next page for your test's exact sectional rules.

5. Language of Question Paper

  • Questions in non-language subjects can be viewed in your chosen language where translation is available.
  • Language Subject Rule: Questions on Language subjects (e.g., English, Hindi, Sanskrit, etc.) will be presented strictly in that language and cannot be translated.
