# Circular Progress Modal & RRB Logo Animation Rules

## Component Design
1. **Glassmorphism Backdrop**: Full-screen modal overlay during AI generation, upload, and export.
2. **Central RRB Logo**: Central logo with glowing scale pulse animation (`rrbPulse`).
3. **SVG Progress Ring**: Animated circular ring representing completion progress (0% to 100%).
4. **Live Percentage & Status Labels**:
   - Numeric counter (`10%`, `20%`, `35%`, `50%`, `75%`, `90%`, `100%`).
   - Dynamic step-by-step progress status text.
